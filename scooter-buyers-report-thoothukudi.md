**Scope:** Top 5 lightweight EV scooters + Top 5 lightweight petrol scooters, with Thoothukudi-specific pricing and dealers wherever published

## READ THIS FIRST: how reliable the data is

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Taken from a 2026 source found during this research (cited in the Sources list) |
| **Est.** | My estimate. The method is explained next to the figure |
| † | Manufacturer spec as I know it, **not re-confirmed** in this round of research. Check it on the brochure or at the showroom |
| ⚠️ | Sources conflict, or something is unconfirmed |

**Limits of this research (please read):**
1. During research, the network blocked direct page loads of BikeWale, Autocar India and the manufacturer dealer locators. All figures therefore come from **search-engine extracts of those pages** (BikeWale, ZigWheels, Bikes24, 91Wheels, Autocar India, Rushlane, TVS/Honda/Chetak sites and others). I could not open the official dealer-locator pages myself to verify them.
2. Dealer names and addresses come from **dealer directories** (BikeWale, Bikes4Sale, CarBike360). They match each brand's dealer listings, but **before you pay any money, confirm the dealer on the brand's official locator** (links are in Part 7).
3. Thoothukudi on-road prices change with the insurer, accessories and "handling" charges. **Treat every on-road price as a starting point for negotiation, not a final quote.**

### Big changes in 2026 that affect your decision
- **PM E-DRIVE central subsidy for e-scooters has ENDED.** It was extended from 31 Mar 2026 to **31 Jul 2026** and then closed. As of August 2026 there was no fresh notification ✅ ([Autocar India](https://www.autocarindia.com/bike-news/pm-e-drive-subsidy-extended-until-july-31-for-electric-two-wheelers-439316), [EVFY](https://www.evfy.in/news/pm-e-drive-subsidy-ends-what-it-means-for-ev-buyers-from-august-2026)). EV prices quoted below include **no central subsidy**.
- **Tamil Nadu still waives 100% of road tax on EVs** registered between 1 Jan 2026 and 31 Dec 2027 ✅ ([The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-ev-road-tax-exemption-till-dec-31-2027-223729)). This saves about ₹8,000–12,000 compared with a petrol scooter of the same price.
- **TN electricity:** no tariff hike for 2026. Domestic slabs run from **₹4.95 to ₹12.15/unit**, and the free-units allowance continues ✅ ([TheDiscomBill](https://thediscombill.com/guides/tamil-nadu-electricity-tariff-july-2026/)).
- **Petrol in Thoothukudi:** **₹101.45/L** (17 Jul 2026, the latest dated figure I found; earlier it was ₹101.13 on 4 Apr 2026) ✅ ([Shriram Finance](https://www.shriramfinance.in/petrol-price-in-thoothukudi), [GoldPriceIndia](https://www.goldpriceindia.com/petrol-price-in-thoothukudi.php)). ⚠️ I could not find a figure dated October 2026. Check the price at the pump; it moves only by paise.

**What this means in practice:** ₹/kg tells you how much "scooter" you get per rupee, but **for you a lower weight is the benefit**, not a cost. A light scooter with a high ₹/kg (Fascino, Orbiter) can still be the better buy. Use ₹/kg mainly to spot outliers:
- The Pleasure+ is unusually cheap for what it is.
- Rizta and iQube carry a premium for their technology and EV hardware.

### "What will I gain by changing from my 10-year-old Pleasure?"
- **Safety:** combined braking (CBS), brighter LED lighting, optional disc brake and side-stand cut-off. This is the single biggest real-world improvement.
- **Reliability and peace of mind:** fuel injection starts first time, there is no carburettor tuning, and a new warranty covers 3–10 years.
- **Lower running cost:** a new FI petrol engine saves ~10% on fuel. An EV cuts the energy cost by **~90%**.
- **Convenience:** bigger boot (Jupiter, Orbiter, C25), USB charging, digital meter, and on some models external fuel filling or a smart key.
- **Emissions/compliance:** BS6 OBD-2B (petrol) or zero tailpipe (EV).

### "What will I lose by moving from my old petrol Pleasure to an EV?"
- **Refuel freedom.** A 2-minute petrol stop becomes a 2–5-hour charge, and home charging is practically required.
- **Mechanic freedom.** Any roadside mechanic can fix a Pleasure; EV faults need the brand workshop.
- **Range for unplanned trips.** 75–90 real km is plenty in town, but tight for a sudden Thoothukudi → Tirunelveli → back day trip (~110 km).
- **Highway pace** on the Chetak C25 (55 km/h cap).
- **Resale certainty.** Used-EV prices depend on battery health and are still unpredictable.
- **A cost cliff after warranty.** A battery replacement costs **₹45,000–60,000** for these pack sizes (2026 estimates) ✅ (EVFY).

### "What will I gain by buying a new petrol scooter instead?"
- **Zero learning curve.** It is the same as the Pleasure, just better.
- **The lowest upfront price** (Pleasure+ from ₹86,556).
- **The strongest resale and the longest usable life** (10+ years is normal for an Activa or Jupiter).
- **Service anywhere** in Thoothukudi district and across TN.
- **No battery-replacement risk**, and no worry about Thoothukudi's summer heat degrading the battery.
- **Downside:** you keep paying ~₹2/km. That is **~₹15,000/yr at 20 km/day** and **~₹37,000/yr at 50 km/day**.

# PART 11 — EV OR PETROL? A CLEAR ANSWER

### My recommendation: **Buy the TVS iQube 2.2 kWh (EV) IF all three of these are true:**
1. You have **a covered parking spot with a 15 A (or a dedicated 5 A) socket** at home.
2. You ride **≤ ~45 km on most days** and rarely do unplanned 100 km+ trips.
3. You are willing to service **only at the TVS authorised workshop**.

**Why:**
- It feels almost exactly like your Pleasure (+6 kg, +5 mm seat), is livelier in traffic and needs no petrol stops.
- It saves **₹45,000–60,000 over 5 years at 20 km/day**, and much more at higher use, *after* allowing for a battery provision.
- TN's road-tax exemption (to Dec 2027) still applies.
- TVS has the deepest dealer bench in Thoothukudi.

### Choose **petrol (Honda Activa 110 or TVS Jupiter 110)** instead IF any of these is true:
- **No home charging.** Thoothukudi has little public EV charging, so do not buy an EV that depends on it.
- You want to keep the scooter **8–10 years with no large one-off bill**. An EV battery replacement of ₹45–60k is likely somewhere in years 6–9.
- You ride very little (**<15 km/day**). The fuel saving then shrinks to ~₹10k/yr, and the petrol scooter's resale and simplicity win.
- You often carry a pillion on the highway, or want to ride to Tiruchendur/Tirunelveli and back on a whim.

🥇 **1st choice: TVS iQube 2.2 kWh** (with the 5 yr/70k battery extension)
- It is the only option that gives you a big running-cost cut *and* a well-supported local network.
- Its weight and seat are close enough to your Pleasure that the switch takes one ride to get used to.
- It is reasonably quick in Thoothukudi traffic.
- It has the lowest 5-year cost among the "safe" EVs.
- **Condition:** you have home charging. If you ride >45 km/day, take the **3.1 kWh** variant.

🥈 **2nd choice: Honda Activa 110 (DLX or H-Smart)**
- **This is the best choice if you cannot charge at home or want a no-surprises 10-year scooter.**
- Same weight and seat as your Pleasure, CBS, TFT display, idle-stop and a warranty extendable to 10 years.
- Best resale value, with Pearl Honda in the city.
- **Alternative:** if you want a 33 L boot and front fuel filling, the **TVS Jupiter 110** is equally sensible.

🥉 **3rd choice: Bajaj Chetak C25**
- **This is the best choice if your budget is under ₹1 lakh but you want an EV.**
- About ₹19,000 cheaper than the iQube, better real range (~90 km), only 108 kg, metal body and a helmet-sized boot.
- It ranks third because of the 55 km/h ceiling and because **no local Chetak sales/service point is confirmed**. Get that confirmed in writing before booking.

### Before you pay — 5-step checklist
1. **Confirm the dealer** on the official brand locator (Part 7) and ask whether **EV service is done in-house in Thoothukudi**.
2. Ask for a **written on-road quote** that separates ex-showroom, registration, insurance and "handling". Refuse handling charges; they are not legitimate.
3. **For an EV:**
   - Get the battery warranty terms in writing (years, km and minimum health %).
   - Buy the extension.
   - Confirm the charger is included in the price.
   - Ask about home-socket inspection.
4. **Compare insurance.** You can buy the OD cover separately from the dealer's insurer and often save ₹1,000–2,000.
5. **Take a test ride with your usual pillion** and put your helmet in the boot.
