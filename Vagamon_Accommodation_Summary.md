## Important caveats

- This research environment's network policy blocked direct page fetches, DNS and SMTP. Every fact was read through the search engine's copy of public pages, not by opening the pages live.
- No mailbox checks were possible, no property was contacted, and no test emails were sent.
- Distances marked "~" or "est." are estimated from the locality. Google Maps links are name searches, not verified map pins.
- No private waterfall is independently confirmed. Public waterfalls near a property are never counted as private.
- A published email does not guarantee the inbox is monitored or that anyone will reply.
