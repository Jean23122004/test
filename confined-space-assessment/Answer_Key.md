## Analysis of the original paper and changes made

1. **Question count.** The instructions said 25 questions, but only 23 were given. The paper now has 25.
2. **Marks did not add up.** 25 questions × 5 marks = 125, so 60 % was not 15 correct. Changed to 4 marks per question (100 in total). Now 60 % = 15 correct.
3. **Questions 8 and 9 had the same wording** ("Which pairing of role and main responsibility is correct?"). Each one now names the roles it tests (new Q6 and Q7).
4. **Answer-position patterns.** In the original, answers to the hierarchy and role questions often fell on the same letter. The options are now shuffled so each letter is correct 6–7 times, with no letter correct three times in a row.
5. **Syllabus gaps.** The original had no questions on *Auxiliary Equipment* (entry & exit, retrieval, fall protection, electrical equipment) or *Permit Durations*. New Q17 and Q23–Q25 cover them.
6. **Questions moved out to keep the total at 25.** These two original questions are kept as reserves:
   - **R1.** Who must be recorded in the confined space entry log? A. Only the supervisor; **B. Every person who enters and leaves the space**; C. Only the first person to enter; D. Only visitors to the site. — **Answer B**
   - **R2.** Who can stop confined space work if they see an unsafe condition? A. Only the Area Authority; B. Only the Performing Authority; **C. Anyone involved in the work**; D. Only the safety officer. — **Answer C**
7. Questions are grouped and ordered by syllabus section. The wording of the 21 original questions kept in the paper is unchanged. Only the option order was changed.
